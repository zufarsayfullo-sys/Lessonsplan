LESSON PLAN & CLASSROOM MATERIALS

Unit 6 · Our Towns and Cities | Beginner Lesson 6.1

1. Teacher Guide & Lesson Flow

This lesson focuses on reading comprehension, urban environments, and targeted city-based vocabulary. Maximize student talking time by letting them analyze the lead-in graphic and read aloud.

Stage Timing Teacher Instructions & Student Activities
Lead-in Map Discussion 5 mins Display the interactive vector city map. Ask students to spot structures (Museum, Clinic, Hotel). Work through the 4 reflection questions to connect ideas to their personal hometown experiences.
Pre-teach Vocabulary 10 mins Utilize the interactive flashcards. Prompt kids to deduce the translation from visual SVGs first. Introduce usage examples and practice pronunciation of target items.
Reading & Analysis 15 mins Have students read the profiles of Lewisburg and Mount Angel. Highlight town demographic metadata comparison cards. Discuss differences between modern and historic towns.
Target Practice Tasks 10 mins Assign Exercise 1 (Underline Town association statements) and Exercise 2 (True/False details validation). Provide instant formative feedback.

2. Reading Handout: Two Towns

Town A: Lewisburg, WEST VIRGINIA

Founded: 1782 | Population: 4,000 | Average age: 46

Jack: I live in Lewisburg, West Virginia, and I love the place. I'm 68 and it's a great place for people like me. There are only 4,000 people in Lewisburg, but it has the facilities of a big town. There are museums, cinemas and good shops. There's a theatre, too, and the famous Carnegie Hall, as well as many other beautiful old buildings. We're very lucky. Oh, and there's an amazing bakery – my favourite shop in Lewisburg. They have great pizza. But one problem is that Lewisburg has a lot of visitors. There are nine different hotels for them!

Town B: Mount Angel, OREGON

Founded: 1850 | Population: 3,400 | Average age: 37

Sara: I live in Mt. Angel, a small town in the lovely Willamette Valley in Oregon. Mt. Angel is famous for its festival – the 'Oktoberfest'. There are four nice restaurants in the town. My favourite is the Glockenspiel. It's a German restaurant. But Mt. Angel doesn't have a lot of facilities. There isn't a hotel, for example. There are banks and supermarkets. But there isn't a cinema and there aren't any museums. There isn't a theatre either. There's a golf course near the town, but I don't play golf. But that's OK, because the country is beautiful and the sea is only two hours away by car.

4. Classroom Exercises Worksheet

Exercise 1: Choose the correct town

Write Lewisburg or Mt. Angel next to each statement:

1. This is a good town for old people. [ ________________________ ]
2. This town has a famous festival. [ ________________________ ]
3. A lot of tourists (visitors) visit here. [ ________________________ ]
4. This town does not have a lot of facilities. [ ________________________ ]
5. This town has famous old buildings. [ ________________________ ]
6. This town is near the sea. [ ________________________ ]

Exercise 2: True or False (Vocabulary Context Practice)

Circle T (True) or F (False) for each statement based on word meaning:

1. If a town has a lot of facilities, it is easy to find things to do. [ T / F ]
2. If something is two hours away, it takes 15 minutes to travel there. [ T / F ]
3. A bakery is a business where people usually play tennis. [ T / F ]
4. If you are lucky, good opportunities and pleasant things happen to you. [ T / F ]
5. Visitors are people who live permanently in a house. [ T / F ]
